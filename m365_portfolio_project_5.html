Key Takeaway — Shared Mailboxes

  • Shared mailboxes allow multiple users to manage a single email address without purchasing an additional licence — ideal for support teams and department inboxes
  • Members with "Send As" permission send emails that appear from the shared address, not their personal account — keeping communications professional and consistent
  • Sent Items are stored in the shared mailbox folder, giving the whole team visibility of outgoing correspondence
  • Testing via two separate Outlook sessions (shared mailbox + recipient) is the correct approach to verify full send-and-receive functionality

Key Takeaway — SharePoint Permissions & Document Management

  • SharePoint's three-group model (Members/Owners/Visitors) maps directly to Edit, Full Control, and Read access — implementing least-privilege principles at the site level
  • Breaking permission inheritance on a folder allows sensitive content (e.g. HR documents) to have a completely separate access control list, invisible to general site members
  • External sharing can be scoped to individual files or folders — administrators control whether links are view-only, editable, or require sign-in
  • SharePoint integrates natively with Microsoft 365 Groups, Teams, and Exchange — a site provisioned from the SharePoint Admin Center automatically creates a corresponding group email and Teams channel
